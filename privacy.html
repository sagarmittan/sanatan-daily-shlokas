Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Sanatan is a spiritual application offering Bhagavad Geeta teachings, audiobooks, sacred books, and daily shlokas.

This policy explains how information may be processed when you use Sanatan.

2. Advertising

Sanatan uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and approximate location to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to applicable privacy requirements and user choices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

3. Privacy Choices

Where required, Sanatan will provide privacy and consent choices for advertising.

Users may also manage certain advertising privacy settings through their device settings.

4. Reading and Listening Progress

Sanatan stores reading progress, audiobook playback positions, and saved shlokas locally on your device.

These preferences help you continue reading and listening where you stopped.

5. Online Content

Sanatan retrieves daily Bhagavad Geeta shlokas and may retrieve additional books and audio content from online hosting providers.

These providers may process technical information necessary to deliver content, such as IP addresses and access requests.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards for information processed through Sanatan. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Sanatan provides spiritual and educational content. We take children's privacy seriously and will apply additional safeguards where required by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when Sanatan's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published here.
